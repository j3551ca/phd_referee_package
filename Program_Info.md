# PhD Programs, Deadlines & Reference Letter Guide

Jessica M. Caleta · Autumn 2027 entry · Updated 28 October 2026

**Deadline:** Please submit both the application and reference letters by the listed deadline unless the notes specify otherwise. Dates without a verified cutoff time are shown as published, not converted from an assumed midnight.

**Time zones:** PT means B.C./Vancouver Pacific time (UTC−7), and CT means Saskatchewan Central Standard Time (UTC−6). These labels reflect the referees’ locations. B.C. now remains on UTC−7, so PT here differs from California’s winter PST (UTC−8). [B.C. time guidance](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/celebrating-british-columbia/daylight-saving-time) · [Saskatchewan time guidance](https://www.timeanddate.com/time/zone/canada/saskatchewan).

**Application status:** Jessica can replace “—” with Not started, In progress, Submitted (date), or Withdrawn. A blank status means not yet recorded, not necessarily unstarted. Application submission and reference submission are separate actions.

**Reference links sent:** Use “Not sent”, “Partly sent (date)”, or “Sent to all (date)” after checking the portal. “Not recorded” means invitation status has not been updated. If a row says “Sent to all” and your invitation is missing, please check spam and contact Jessica for a resend. This tracks invitations, not completed letters.
